## Local adaptation

`explain-clearly` replaces this repository's `technical-writing` entry point
and condenses the relevant responsibilities of the separately installed
`simple-english` and `clarity` workflows. Meaning-preserving language rules take
the place of blanket vocabulary, modal-verb, and sentence-count restrictions.
The existing documentation resources are condensed into `documentation.md`.
Original explainer guidance adds learning-oriented visual composition,
interaction, typography, and rendered verification without requiring a second
writing or design skill. The external foundations and their limits are listed
in `sources.md`; their full texts are not vendored here.
